# VSRG Converter

A Geometry Dash mod that parses VSRG (Vertical Scrolling Rhythm Game) macros and visualizes them natively as a falling note overlay while you play! 

## Features
- **Auto-Offset Syncing**: Automatically reads the song offset of your level and aligns the notes perfectly.
- **Dynamic Key Splitting**: Notes automatically split from 1-key to 2-key, 3-key, and 4-key layouts based on click density (CPS).
- **Customizable Scroll Speed**: Change the scroll speed globally or per-map using custom keybinds.
- **Custom Skinning**: Change note appearances by creating custom sprite folders.
- **In-Game Macro Import**: Import `.gdr.json` macros directly from the level page.
- **Per-Map Settings**: Your custom offset adjustments and keybinds are saved automatically for each map.
